## Needs a decision
- **Automatic name and cover for books, taken from Ereadian (the app no longer has any book data source).** Blocked as a static page: the browser refuses to read ereadian.com from our origin (no CORS headers; checked for search and book pages, and a `no-cors` request comes back opaque with no body or headers, the same for found and not found), the search page cannot be framed (`X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN`), its cover URLs are signed (`/img?...&s=...&k=...`, 403 without the signature) and `robots.txt` disallows `/img`, search URLs and `/api/`; its terms are unpublished. For now the user types a name on the unknown card. Options: ask Ereadian for an API or CORS access; a bookmarklet the user installs that reads the first result on the Ereadian tab and opens our app at `#isbn=…&title=…&cover=…` (accept only `https://ereadian.com/img?` covers, render via `textContent`); a local proxy (scraping, against the "no backend" rule, not recommended).
- **Lookup service: deploy and settle the open points.** Built 2026-10-26 (`worker/`, `src/lookup.js`, `src/meta.js`; design and Goodreads findings are in `worker/README.md` and `CLAUDE.md`); deployed the same day at `https://isbn-lookup.oleprota.workers.dev` and checked live (Goodreads does answer Cloudflare's addresses). Done 2026-10-26: the card shows series, first author and rating, and the title has its brackets cut. Still to decide: read Goodreads' terms of service; a series link to the series' own Goodreads page (now a search for its name; the page's HTML has `/series/<id>-<slug>` links, but the lookup would have to read much more of the page); a book Goodreads doesn't know answered an empty 202 earlier (9789661092999), but later gave a full page, so answers can change.
- **Fallback-assisted lookup for books Goodreads doesn't know by ISBN** (new reprints; example 9786171519992 "Хірург", Тесс Ґеррітсен: `goodreads.com/book/isbn/…` answers a real 404, but the Ukrainian edition exists as `/book/show/32740697`). Not building it now. The idea, all inside the worker (the app and its card stay as they are): when Goodreads answers a certain 404, (1) ask a web-search API for "ISBN <number>" and guess title and author from the top results' titles (a shop page title looks like "Хірург. Книга 1 - Тесс Ґеррітсен"); (2) search Goodreads for "title author" (`goodreads.com/search?q=Хірург Тесс Ґеррітсен` found exactly that edition; the author's spelling matters: «Ґеррітсен» found it, «Геррітсен» found nothing, so try both and drop a trailing ". Книга N" from the title); (3) accept a result only when its title and author match after cleaning, then load its page for the rating, series and cover, else show just the guessed name and author; (4) cache these answers shorter than real ones. **Source: Brave Search API** (https://brave.com/search/api/): $5 per 1,000 searches with $5 of free credit a month, a credit card is required even for the free credit, the key goes in as a worker secret (`wrangler secret put`), and the worker needs a hard daily cap (a small KV counter, for example 30 searches a day) so a flood of made-up valid ISBNs can't run up a bill. A one-off look at Brave's results page (2026-10-26) did find three Ukrainian shops for that ISBN (book.ua, knyharnia.eu, mbooks.com.ua); the API itself is untested, and Brave's terms on storing results are unclear (our cache keeps answers up to 7 days). Ruled out (2026-10-26): scraping Google, DuckDuckGo or Bing from the worker (JavaScript wall, bot check, no readable results, against their terms); Google's Custom Search JSON API (closed to new customers, ends 1 January 2027, no whole-web search); LangSearch (free and no card, but it searches by meaning: unrelated books for four Ukrainian ISBNs, nothing about Хірург even with the title and author in the query); Open Library (empty for this ISBN); Ereadian (knows the book, with author and a Goodreads rating, but its robots.txt forbids search URLs). Still to try: the Google Books API (a free key, structured title and authors, coverage of Ukrainian reprints unknown). `CLAUDE.md` would need a line allowing a book-data source inside the worker only.
