# Verify scanning

Read `../verify-app/common.md` first (setup, closing check, report). Headless Chromium has no camera: `Read` `../verify-app/fake-camera.js` and pass its arrow function to `page.evaluate`. Control it with `window.__setBarcode('<ean13>' | null)`. Start from clean `localStorage`.

1. **Camera denied.** On a fresh load `vendor/zxing-browser.min.js` was *not* requested (the camera must never start by itself). Before clicking Scan, simulate a denial: `navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia = () => Promise.reject(new DOMException('Permission denied', 'NotAllowedError'))`. Click Scan: status `S.scanner.cameraUnavailable` (error style), `#scanner` hidden, field and Search enabled, button `S.scanner.scan`, field focused. (This yields the one expected console error.)
2. **Gestures.** A tap on `#scan` is a single scan; holding (`page.mouse.down()`, ~1.3 s, `up()`) is Keep scanning, ended by the Stop tap. The red fill (`holding`) starts after 0.2 s and takes 0.8 s; a press under 0.2 s is a plain tap; releasing during the fill only cancels it (no scan, no Stop).
3. **Single scan, new book.** `__setBarcode('9780747532699')`, click Scan, wait ~0.7 s (the camera stays open 300 ms after the first picture): camera stopped (scanner hidden, `S.scanner.scan`), field holds the ISBN, hash set, card `ISBN 9780747532699` with ☆, status empty, Scanned count 1, Marked empty.
4. **Preview.** While open with barcode `null`: `#view` has painted pixels (alpha 255 in the middle), is as wide as the page content (`main` width minus its padding) and 4:3, or capped at 50vh high when the viewport is shorter than 960 px (so use a viewport at least 960 px high to see 4:3); `<video id="preview">` is 1×1 px. After Stop the canvas is transparent again; a restart creates a *new* `#preview`. Stop right after a read, inside the 300 ms wait, adds nothing.
5. **Single scan, repeat.** Scan the same ISBN again after ~3.2 s: status `S.lists.alreadyScanned("ISBN 9780747532699")`, still one row. A *typed* search of a scanned ISBN shows the card with no message.
6. **Keep scanning.** Barcode `9780140328721`, hold Scan ~1.3 s: Scanned has it on top at once, status `S.lists.added("ISBN 9780140328721")`, camera stays on (button `S.scanner.stop`); field, Search, both Clear buttons and the list names are disabled; Scanned (collapsed while empty) opens itself; the previous card is gone. Same barcode for ~4.5 s: still one new row. `9780606186513`: another row on top. Non-book EAN-13 `4006381333931`: `S.search.notABook`, nothing added; `null`: the message clears within a couple of seconds. Show the first book again after ~3.5 s of `null` (a sighting within 3 s of the last counts as the same): `S.lists.alreadyScanned("ISBN 9780140328721")`, count unchanged, row to the top; the message appears once, stays while the barcode stays in view, clears ~2 s after it leaves. A just-added book keeps `S.lists.added(…)` while in view. Stop: scanner hidden, `S.scanner.scan`, field enabled. Nothing is marked by scanning.
7. **Full Scanned list.** With 1000 entries stored, a scan of a new book shows the card (single) or `S.lists.notAdded(title)` in error style (Keep scanning); warning visible, nothing added, red page flash with a 330 Hz beep (no further beeps while the barcode stays in view).
8. **Beep and flash.** Wrap `AudioContext.prototype.createOscillator` in an init script to record `frequency.value`, and watch `#snap`'s classes (`flash`, plus `again`). New book: one 880 Hz record, amber flash. Already scanned: 520 Hz and `again` (blue). Full Scanned list (check 7): 330 Hz and class `full flash`. Non-book barcode or a repeat inside the duplicate window: nothing. The colours (amber, blue, red) are not checked headless: only the class names. The sound itself is "not verified" headless.

Finish with the closing check from `common.md`.
