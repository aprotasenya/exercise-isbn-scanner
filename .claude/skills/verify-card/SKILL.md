# Verify the book card

Read `../verify-app/common.md` first (setup, closing check, report). Search form is `#search`; a search is instant.

1. **Card.** Search `9780747532699`: status empty; `.book` with heading `ISBN 9780747532699`; `h2.book-title` holds a button `.title-edit` (tooltip `S.card.editName`) that looks like the plain title with a thin (1 px) dashed underline, always visible and set clear of the letters' descenders (computed `text-decoration-style: dashed`, `text-decoration-thickness` 1px, and `text-underline-offset` not less than the value in `style.css` (`.22em`; the computed value is in px, so compare with 0.22 × the button's computed `font-size`)); `.mark-toggle` shows ☆ (`aria-pressed="false"`, tooltip/`aria-label` `S.card.mark`); `.link-row` has two `.link-button`s (Ereadian, Goodreads) with loaded 128 px PNG icons from `icons/`, opening in a new tab (`rel="noopener noreferrer"`, tooltip `S.card.linkTitle(isbn, site)`) at `https://ereadian.com/search?q=9780747532699` and `https://www.goodreads.com/book/isbn/9780747532699`. No rating, authors, cover or "More by…". Hash is `#isbn=9780747532699`; Scanned (open via `#scanned summary`) holds one row. The card's series and author links (`.facts a`) are underlined like the list names (see `verify-lists`, check 5). Repeat for other ISBNs, including an ISBN-10 (`080442957X`) and a Ukrainian one.
   - Dark mode (`page.emulateMedia({ colorScheme: 'dark' })`): wait ~0.5 s after switching (buttons fade their background over 0.15 s), take a screenshot, **open it with `Read` and judge it**: both icons clearly visible and every button label readable; keyboard focus on a button shows a 2 px outline.
   - **Geometry guards** (desktop and 360 px): no sideways scroll; the star's right edge equals the ✕ right edge in the Marked list; the star's top is level with the heading's top, also for a very long name that wraps.
2. **Language.** `<html lang="uk">`; no English in the interface text; allow site names, `ISBN` and the `ID` in the stats note. Book data is never checked for language: typed names, found titles, series and authors (from the lookup worker) may be in any language. Check wrapping at 360 px. (That the static texts match `strings.js` is `verify-strings`.)
3. **Editing the title.** Tap `.title-edit`: `.title-input` replaces it, focused, text selected, `aria-label` `S.card.nameLabel`, placeholder `ISBN <isbn>`. Type `My Ukrainian book` + Enter: heading, Scanned row and (once starred) Marked entry show it; `localStorage['isbn-reader.names']` holds it; a reload keeps it. Escape cancels; blur saves. Clearing (or only spaces) falls back to `ISBN <isbn>` everywhere and removes the stored name. Enter without changes, or typing exactly `ISBN <isbn>`, stores nothing. A legacy title (a "Matilda" row) is kept when opened and unchanged.
   - **Geometry guard:** the text does not shift sideways when the editor opens: the field's left edge plus its border and padding (read from computed style) equals the left edge of `h2.book-title`, and the field is as wide as the heading plus that border and padding on both sides (the field box is deliberately wider than the heading by that amount). It is single-line.
   - **Keyboard regression:** Tab to the title button, Enter opens the editor; type, Enter closes it and it *stays* closed (Enter must not reopen it) with focus on the title button; Space opens it too; Escape closes without saving and without reopening; leaving by tapping elsewhere does not steal focus. (Headless Chromium on Mac: text is preselected, just type; `Control+A` moves the cursor.)
4. **Focus.** The ISBN field is never auto-focused (with and without `#isbn=…`; also after opening a book from a list): on load `document.activeElement` is the body, and after any search or list click it is not `#isbn`. A valid typed search leaves `#isbn` unfocused (the phone keyboard closes; a clicked Search button may keep focus) and shows the floating Scan controls; an invalid one keeps the field usable.
5. **Invalid ISBN.** Search `1234567890`: status `S.search.invalidIsbn`, empty result, nothing added to Scanned. The message clears on the next edit of the field; `S.lists.copied(…)` clears itself after ~4 s; error messages stay until an edit or the next action.
6. **Deep links.** Load `#isbn=9780747532699` and reload: card, field filled, hash unchanged. Search `9780141365466`: hash becomes `#isbn=9780141365466`. `page.goBack()` shows the first card again, field filled, `history.length` not increased. Load `#isbn=1234567890` and reload: no search runs.
7. **Theme toggle.** `#theme` cycles the labels `S.theme.labels.auto` → `.light` → `.dark` → auto. Compare the computed `body` background against `--bg` in `style.css` (light/dark). Auto (no `data-theme`, nothing stored) follows `emulateMedia` live both ways; Light/Dark override the device; a reload keeps Dark (`isbn-reader.theme` = `dark`); back to Auto removes the key.

Finish with the closing check from `common.md`.
